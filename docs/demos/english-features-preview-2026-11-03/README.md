# English feature preview, 2026-11-03

This preview is for review; customer acceptance remains pending. The [homepage preview](../english-homepage-preview-2026-11-03/README.md) shows the complete Chinese homepage layout, English additions, Community, language entries and navigation on desktop and mobile. The earlier [English preview](../english-preview-2026-11-02/README.md) and [theme preview](../english-theme-preview-2026-11-03/README.md) are historical and do not show the current visitor design.

The demonstration uses the Chinese archive dated 2026-09-24: 225 products, 1,079 gallery images and 22 Community articles. English text is an unapproved demonstration translation; some original artwork contains Chinese. The archive does not establish current live content, and product specifications, safety and marketing claims have not been verified. Historical contact details may no longer be valid; do not use them for live contact. There are 103 unavailable inline file paths: 98 images and five PDFs. Five further printable-step references have instruction text but no source link. News, video covers and distributors include labelled illustrative examples, excluded from the archive counts.

All pictures below show the demonstration on 2026-11-03. Recordings can be downloaded. A guided local interactive demonstration is available during review. Selecting a YouTube cover activates its player; the appearance or launch of an external player is not part of visual review. A quotation link prepares an email with the product, model and page; its external mail application is not part of visual review, and the demonstration sends no message.
