# English preview, 2026-11-02

This demonstration uses the Chinese archive dated 2026-09-24. It includes 225 products and 1,079 product-image relationships. English is demonstration translation, not approved final copy; 103 inline illustrations are omitted, and some model and image text remains Chinese.

Archived contact details, specifications, warranty and safety information are demonstration content and require review before current use. DEMO News, video covers and distributors are illustrative, not historical records.

Desktop and phone-sized views are browser viewports, not physical devices. The YouTube Developers video is a public test example, not a KiKAiTO product video. A prefilled native mail application and physical phone actions remain unverified; no message was sent and no call was placed. The archived Chinese homepage has horizontal overflow in the tested phone-sized viewport. Customer review and acceptance remain pending.

## Feature overview

- **Products and categories** ([#4](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/4), [#5](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/5), [#7](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/7), [#11](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/11), [#12](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/12)): administrators edit English product details, images and category selections, preview drafts and publish. See product, category and editor captures below.
- **Homepage, videos and News** ([#6](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/6), [#8](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/8), [#13](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/13), [#18](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/18)): administrators manage featured items, icons, video covers and News. See homepage, video and News captures.
- **Information and enquiries** ([#9](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/9), [#10](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/10), [#14](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/14)): administrators edit information pages and set the enquiry recipient. The email client result remains pending.
- **Where to Buy and Chinese pages** ([#15](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/15), [#16](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/16), [#17](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/17), [#18](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/18)): administrators manage distributor details and order; visitors can return to existing Chinese pages.

# 英文預覽，2026-11-02

此示範使用 2026-09-24 中文封存資料，包含 225 項商品及 1,079 筆商品與圖片關聯。英文為示範翻譯，並非已核准正式文案；103 個內嵌示意圖未納入，部分型號與圖片文字仍為中文。

封存中的聯絡資料、規格、保固及安全資訊僅供示範，作為現行內容使用前須核對。標示 DEMO 的 News、影片封面及經銷商為示範資料，並非歷史紀錄。

桌機及手機大小畫面來自瀏覽器視窗，並非實體裝置。YouTube Developers 影片是公開測試範例，並非 KiKAiTO 商品影片。原生郵件程式預填及實體電話操作仍未驗證；未寄信或撥號。封存中文首頁在測試的手機大小視窗有橫向溢出。客戶檢視及驗收仍待完成。
