# English theme demonstration, 2026-11-03

This demonstration shows English pages using the Chinese theme, style and colours. The component styles match; translated content and available modules differ, and Chinese featured selections are random. The screenshots show the demonstration version dated 2026-11-03, using the Chinese source archive dated 2026-09-24. A guided local interactive demo is available during review; the screenshots below remain available in this guide.

The archive supplies 225 translated products and 1,079 product images; 103 distinct archived inline illustrations are missing. The English translation is demonstration copy and has not been approved as final. The archive does not establish current production content. Product specifications, safety and marketing claims are unverified. Historical contact details may no longer be valid and must not be used for live contact. News, video and distributor examples are illustrative; the distributors are not real.

External YouTube playback has not been verified. Local intercepted checks cover the embedded player layout, not external playback. The existing Chinese mobile page can overflow the screen width. Owner visual verification and actual customer acceptance remain separate and pending.

## Visitor pages

Use the navigation to open categories, series and products, then choose a gallery image or the email quotation entry. The quotation entry provides an email draft link; actual mail application launch remains unverified. No email was sent. Select News for its list and article, or use the information and Where to Buy links. The Chinese link returns to the Chinese site.
