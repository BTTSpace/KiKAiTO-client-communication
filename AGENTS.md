# Acceptance evidence

- Every applicable issue submitted for completion or acceptance in both the development and customer communication repositories must have its own comment directly embedding actual screenshots matching that issue's requirements. A general guide link or an unrelated generic image alone is insufficient.
- Before treating evidence as ready, inspect the actual images at readable scale against the issue requirements and demonstrated version, theme, alignment, and applicable before/after and desktop/mobile states. Record the inspected images, version and results in the appropriate evidence; keep technical details private. For image assessment, use Claude Code first; if it cannot respond or complete the assessment, use AGY (Antigravity), then Codex if AGY also cannot respond or complete it. Assessors only inspect images or recording frames and provide opinions; Terra implements corrections and the coordinator verifies findings and resolution. A manifest or passing test alone does not establish image correctness. Missing, mismatched or unreviewed images keep readiness pending.
- Retain applicable functional and recording evidence for interactions. The coordinator directly inspects the images and checks the assessment and any corrections before evidence is ready.
- Under the [owner policy of 2026-11-04](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/2#issuecomment-6609262373), close an issue after the work is complete and required checks, reviews, demonstrations, evidence and report are verified. Keep unfinished work open or explicitly tracked in a linked follow-up. Customer confirmation is recorded separately when received and does not block closure; completion, images, reviews and merges do not establish customer acceptance. For a later problem, reopen the original issue or create a new issue that explicitly names and links all related original issues and states the problem and completion checks.

# 驗收證據

- 開發與客戶溝通兩個倉庫中，每個適用且提交完成或驗收的議題，都必須在該議題留言直接嵌入符合自身需求的實際截圖；只附總指南連結或無關的通用圖片不足以驗收。
- 認定證據備妥前，須以可閱讀大小直接檢查圖片是否符合議題需求、展示版本、主題、對齊及適用的前後狀態與桌機／手機畫面。於適當證據紀錄所檢圖片、版本及結果，技術細節留在私下。圖片評估依序先用 Claude Code；若無法回應或完成評估，改用 AGY（Antigravity）；AGY 亦無法回應或完成時再用 Codex。評估者只直接檢視圖片或錄影影格並提供意見；由 Terra 實作修正，協調者核對發現與處理結果。素材清單或測試通過不能單獨證明圖片正確；圖片缺失、不符或未審查時仍待備妥。
- 互動功能保留適用的功能及錄影證據。證據備妥前，協調者須直接檢視圖片並核對評估及修正結果。
- 依 [2026-11-04 負責人規則](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client-communication/issues/2#issuecomment-6609262373)，工作完成且必要檢查、審查、展示、證據及回報均已核對後，關閉議題。未完成工作須保持開啟，或明確記錄於有連結的後續議題。客戶實際確認收到後另行記錄，且不阻擋結案；工作完成、圖片、審查及合併均不代表客戶驗收。後續發現問題時，重新開啟原議題，或新增議題明確列出並連結所有相關原議題，說明問題及完成檢查。
