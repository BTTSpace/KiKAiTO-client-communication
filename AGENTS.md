# Acceptance evidence

- Every applicable issue submitted for acceptance in both the development and customer communication repositories must have its own comment directly embedding actual screenshots matching that issue's requirements. A general guide link or an unrelated generic image alone is insufficient.
- Before treating evidence as ready, inspect the actual images at readable scale against the issue requirements and demonstrated version, theme, alignment, and applicable before/after and desktop/mobile states. Record the inspected images, version and results in the appropriate evidence; keep technical details private. Orca orchestration may use Claude Code for image review; the coordinator verifies its findings and their resolution. A manifest or passing test alone does not establish image correctness. Missing, mismatched or unreviewed images keep readiness pending.
- Retain applicable functional and recording evidence for interactions. Images, reviews and merges do not equal customer acceptance; keep issues open until actual customer confirmation.

# 驗收證據

- 開發與客戶溝通兩個倉庫中，每個適用且提交驗收的議題，都必須在該議題留言直接嵌入符合自身需求的實際截圖；只附總指南連結或無關的通用圖片不足以驗收。
- 認定證據備妥前，須以可閱讀大小直接檢查圖片是否符合議題需求、展示版本、主題、對齊及適用的前後狀態與桌機／手機畫面。於適當證據紀錄所檢圖片、版本及結果，技術細節留在私下。Orca orchestration 可由 Claude Code 執行圖片審查，協調者須核對其發現及處理結果。素材清單或測試通過不能單獨證明圖片正確；圖片缺失、不符或未審查時仍待備妥。
- 互動功能保留適用的功能及錄影證據；圖片、審查或合併均不等於客戶驗收，客戶實際確認前議題保持開啟。
